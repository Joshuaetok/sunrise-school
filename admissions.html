Admissions Criteria

Entry Requirements

Select your child's age group to review required documents and academic benchmarks.

Nursery

Ages 2 - 5
  • Birth Certificate copy
  • Immunization records & medical report
  • Two (2) recent passport photographs
  • Satisfactory developmental physical check

Primary

Ages 6 - 11
  • Academic reports from last school (minimum 1 year)
  • Transfer certificate or reference
  • Cognitive diagnostic readiness assessment
  • Two (2) passport photographs

Secondary

JSS1 - SSS3
  • Previous school transcripts (2 years minimum)
  • Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) results where applicable
  • Rigorous entrance exam in English, Mathematics, and Science
  • Interview with Principal

Admissions FAQs

Have questions about the application cycle? Here are quick, definitive answers.

How do I purchase the admission form?

→

Forms can be bought for N15,000 on our secure online portal, or physically at our administrative office across any of our three campuses.

What subjects are tested in the Secondary school entry exam?

→

We test candidates in basic English Language, Mathematics, and general sciences to assess logical reasoning and curriculum readiness.

Do you offer boarding facilities?

→

Yes, our Lagos campus offers modern, highly secure, and comfortable boarding houses for both male and female secondary students.

Is there a sibling discount available?

→

We provide a 10% tuition discount for the second child, and a 15% discount for subsequent siblings enrolled concurrently.
