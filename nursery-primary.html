Early Years Foundation (Ages 1 – 5)

Nursery & Crèche: Learning through joyful play

Our Early Years programme blends the British Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) with Montessori-inspired sensory discovery so every young child feels safe, loved, and eager to explore.

→
Jolly Phonics & Early Literacy
Storytelling, songs, and structured phonics that build early reading confidence and expressive speech.
→
Hands-On Numeracy & Sensory Play
Counting games, shape sorting, blocks, water play, and puzzles that wire young minds for mathematical thinking.
→
Social, Emotional & Motor Development
Sharing, kindness, fine-motor arts and crafts, and supervised outdoor play on cushioned play areas.

Primary School (Ages 5 – 11)

Strong foundations in literacy, STEM, and character

In Primary School, learners transition smoothly from guided discovery to independent inquiry. Our dual Nigerian and Cambridge Primary framework prepares pupils for Common Entrance and Junior Secondary success.

→
Core Reading, Writing & Public Speaking
Weekly reading clubs, creative writing workshops, spelling bees, and elocution practice.
→
Mental Maths, Science & Early Coding
Practical science experiments, Scratch coding, robotics introduction, and problem-solving labs.
→
French, Music, Arts & Civic Values
Well-rounded exposure to international languages, music instruments, visual arts, and Nigerian heritage.
Stage-by-Stage Progression

Tailored learning for every age group

Each stage is structured around age-appropriate milestones, caring class teachers, and specialist instructors.

Ages 1 – 2

Crèche & Playgroup

A warm, hygienic, home-away-from-home nursery with attentive caregivers, sensory toys, music, and gentle routines.

Ages 3 – 5

Nursery 1, 2 & Reception

Phonics, number recognition, nature walks, imaginative role-play, and school readiness habits.

Ages 5 – 8

Lower Primary (Basic 1–3)

Fluent reading comprehension, foundational arithmetic, basic science discovery, French, and creative arts.

Ages 8 – 11

Upper Primary (Basic 4–6)

Advanced problem-solving, ICT & coding, leadership prefectship, and National Common Entrance preparation.

Daily Rhythm & Pastoral Care

A typical day at Seeds of Knowledge Nursery & Primary

Structured to balance focused morning lessons with active play, creative clubs, and restful care.

7:45 AM – 8:30 AM

Warm Welcome & Assembly

Morning greetings, songs, character reflection, and daily readiness check-in with class teachers.

8:30 AM – 11:30 AM

Core Literacy & Numeracy

Interactive phonics, English comprehension, mathematics workshops, and fruit/snack break.

11:30 AM – 1:30 PM

Science, ICT & Outdoor Play

Hands-on inquiry in science, digital literacy, lunch break, and supervised playground recreation.

1:30 PM – 3:00 PM

Arts, Clubs & After-School Care

Music, drama, chess, swimming, homework support, and safe dismissal or school bus departure.
